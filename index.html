Project

Clawsome Connect

An app idea I presented at my Class XII entrepreneurship seminar. Its slogan is “Use Clawsome to feel awesome.” The aim is a safe community for people who are socially anxious or afraid of being judged, where you talk to a real person, not an AI. Logo designed in Canva.

Person to person: you fill in a form about how you feel and what you’re afraid of, and the app connects you with someone who feels similar. You can stay anonymous.

Crew to person: you choose someone from the crew to talk to and vent. Crew members must be 17 or older, and they earn some money for it, which also gives students a way to earn.

Revenue plan: affiliate marketing, in-app donations and native advertising.
